Which plan is right for me?

15 minutes suits one direct question. 30 minutes gives detailed guidance on one important question. 45 minutes covers your full Kundli in depth, along with upay.

Is payment safe?

Yes. Payment happens on Razorpay's secure page. We never store your card or UPI details.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Share whatever you know, even an approximate time. Acharya will explain what guidance is possible with it.

Do I have to fill the form before paying?

No, every field is optional. You can pay first and send your details on WhatsApp.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your birth details and conversation are kept confidential.
